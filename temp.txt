In the indexing mode, numbers are assigned in increasing order (<span class=\"variable-text-block-span-1\">top to bottom</span>) from the parent layer, provided that the parent layer is located above all the child layers. If the parent layer is located below all the child layers, the numbers are assigned in increasing order (<span class=\"variable-text-block-span-1\">bottom to top</span>). If the parent layer is located somewhere between the child layers, the first condition for the numbering order (<span class=\"variable-text-block-span-1\">increasing from top to bottom</span>) is applied.
